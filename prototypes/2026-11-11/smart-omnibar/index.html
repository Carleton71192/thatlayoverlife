The Smart Travel Omnibar

Type a place. See who went.

One bar for the whole library. It answers while you type, and when it has nothing it says so and offers three places it does have.

A city or an airport code works. So does a contributor's name. Arrow keys move through the list and Enter opens the row.

How it works

What is behind the bar.

Stories, live today

Every live story, matched on its title, place, country and contributor. Type it without the accents and São Paulo still turns up. A word that only appears inside a story counts too, and the row says which word.

Layover guides, live today

Both of them, which is all of them for now.

    Expat hubs, live today

    Opening city by city, where our members live. Until a hub has a page, its row in the bar is a label and never a link.

    When nothing matches

    The search no longer ends on an error page. The bar checks your spelling first, then offers places drawn at random from the stories. TLL has no numbers on what people read most, so the picks are random and the label says so.
