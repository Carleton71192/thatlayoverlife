The short version

Most of the parts already exist. The four free mistakes and the rows of the paid guide are live on /the-paw-passport, and the home page carries Nancy's line with a buy button that sits in the page, hidden. Nothing yet leads from the places Magnus actually appears to the guide, and nothing brings a buyer back once Ko-fi has done its job. The plan adds two quiet doors, puts the price and the button inside the guide block, and it leaves the usual pressure tactics out on purpose, since a magazine that tells you the truth about border paperwork cannot then lie to you about stock levels.

        03 · Every timing at or under 250 ms, transform and opacity only

        Micro-interactions

        Small and few. Nothing starts invisible or bounces, and under reduced motion each one becomes an instant change. "Play" runs it in the wireframe above.

        M1 · Doors A and B

        Text link arrow

        On hover or keyboard focus the arrow moves 4 px to the right. The 2 px #C8325B underline does not change.

        transform: translateX(4px)
        250 ms ease-out
        reduced motion: no movement

        M2 · Arrival at #guide

        The block says hello once

        Only when the reader arrives through a door (the address has #guide). The page scrolls to the block, and the block's header rises from 8 px below at 0.6 opacity. A plain visit to /the-paw-passport gets no animation.

        opacity .6 → 1, translateY(8px) → 0
        250 ms ease-out, once
        reduced motion: jump, no fade

        M3 · Buy button

        Button states

        Hover swaps the fill from #D63859 to #C8325B with no transition. Press nudges it down 1 px. Focus on the dark card is a 3 px #00C9C8 ring with a 2 px gap.

        press: translateY(1px), 100 ms
        hover: color swap, instant
        focus: outline, instant

        M4 · The click

        The note under the button changes

        "On Ko-fi · opens in a new tab" becomes "Ko-fi is open in a new tab." The new line fades up from 0.6. The button stays enabled and keeps its label. Screen readers hear the new line once (aria-live polite).

        opacity .6 → 1
        250 ms ease-out
        events sent with transport beacon

        M5 · Tab regained

        The old tab notices

        When the TLL tab becomes visible again, at least 5 seconds after the click, the note becomes "Back already? If the PDF is yours, your dog can have a passport page too." with "Register your pet →". Once per page view.

        opacity .6 → 1, translateY(8px) → 0
        250 ms ease-out
        wait: 5 s, not an animation

        M6 · From Ko-fi

        The thank-you band

        With ?from=kofi in the address, a cream band appears above the block. It does not take focus or scroll the page. "Close" removes it for the rest of the visit.

        opacity .6 → 1, translateY(8px) → 0
        250 ms ease-out
        close: instant

        M7 · Jump link

        "see what is in it ↓"

        Scrolls to the dark card with the guide's contents, price and button. Smooth scroll uses the browser's own timing; under reduced motion it jumps.

        scroll-behavior: smooth
        reduced motion: auto

        Left still on purpose

        The price and the lists

        The price does not count up and the button does not pulse. The free mistakes and the paid rows are text to read, and they are all there at rest.

        04 · For the landing block

        Design triggers, the honest kind

        Every one of these persuades by telling the reader something true and specific. Tick them off as Nancy confirms them; the tally counts from the list. The pin number shows where each one sits in the wireframe.

        Left out on purpose

        • Countdowns and "price goes up Friday". There is no deadline, and inventing one would be the first lie on a page about following rules.
        • "Only a few left". It is a PDF. There are always a few left.
        • Buyer counts, "most popular", star ratings. There are no sales yet to count, and Ko-fi's numbers stay on Ko-fi until there is something true to say.
        • Testimonials and review quotes. None exist. If buyers later write in, Nancy can ask to quote them by name.
        • A crossed-out higher price. The guide has only ever been .
        • Exit pop-ups and a buy bar that follows the reader down the page. The button already sits right under what it buys, and a friend who recommends a book does not follow you to the register.
        • Pre-ticked extras at checkout. Ko-fi checkout gets one item.

        05 · Two events planned, the rest proposed

        What to measure

        Events only send after a reader accepts analytics, so every number here is a floor. Ko-fi's own sales count is the real figure, read by hand.

        guide_purchase_click

        Planned, not built

        Any "Get the guide · €9" click. Params: placement (paw_guide_block or home_ch04), entry (story_end, pet_page or direct, from the door the reader used this visit), value 9, currency EUR. The footer script today sends guide_checkout_click for the same click; keep one name, not both.

        kofi_click

        Live

        Any link to Ko-fi, including the tip jar on /support. Add link_url so guide clicks and tips can be told apart. The same click also sends revenue_click from the division layer, so never add these three together.

        guide_teaser_click

        Proposed

        A click on door A or B. Params: placement, story (slug, door A only). Shows which door earns its place.

        guide_block_view

        Proposed

        Half the #guide block on screen, once per page view. The denominator for the click rate.

        guide_return

        Proposed

        /the-paw-passport loaded with ?from=kofi. A floor for completed purchases, since only buyers see the thank-you link.

        Ko-fi sales

        By hand

        Ko-fi's dashboard, once a week. Not an event; Ko-fi is not on TLL's analytics.

        06 · Before any of this goes near the site

        Nancy decides

        1. The Ko-fi product link. A ko-fi.com/s/ address, not the profile. Until then the button points at KOFI URL FROM NANCY.
        2. The price and the tax line. is the figure given; it is shown in one place in the code and formatted everywhere else. Whether to show "incl. 25% moms" is hers.
        3. The regulation. The brief allows Regulation (EU) No 576/2013. The repo's pet notes (docs/notes-2026-10-31/linkedin-pet-posts.md, citing EUR-Lex) and the prepared tll-pet-passport-v1.js say Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131 replaced it from 22 April 2026, so the block cites 2026/131 and links to it. The live page names neither regulation yet. Confirm, and give the date the guide was last checked against it.
        4. Facts about the PDF. Page count, and whether "free updates when the rules change" (in the prepared code) is a promise she wants to make.
        5. Delivery and the way back. Confirm Ko-fi sends the PDF by email as well as the download, and that the shop item's thank-you message can carry the ?from=kofi link.
        6. A refund line. Only if she wants one. The row is empty until she writes it.
        7. Magnus's country count. Once the site's scripts run, every page agrees on : his page, the example card, the guide intro and home. The block counts the same list from his page. The Webflow HTML underneath still says 12 on his page (with a different list that includes Montenegro), 3 on the example card and 13 in the guide intro, and that older version is what search engines and readers without scripts see. Worth fixing at the source.
        8. One event name. guide_purchase_click (planned) or guide_checkout_click (live). Same click, so keep one.
        9. Which stories get door A. The list in step 01 is the border trips I could confirm Magnus was on. She may want more, or fewer.
        10. The photo. The block uses the hero of "Even Brave Boys Get Scared" (Photo: Jay Carleton) because it is Magnus, clearly. A border photo of him would suit the guide better if one exists.
        11. Two pages for "look inside". Optional, and the most useful thing missing.
