The layover bridge

A small block that turns up between two sections of a story, right where a reader has decided they like the place. It asks whether the country goes on their map, and whether they are only ever going to see its airport.

Pick a story, then scroll. The block arrives at the end of the story’s first section, just before its second heading.

The component

The markup, as built

This is the block exactly as the page renders it for the story you picked, with Tailwind classes on the brand tokens configured at the top of this file. The behavior lives in one small script at the bottom of the file, wired to the data attributes, so the markup carries no inline handlers.
