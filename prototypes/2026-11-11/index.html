That Layover Life · Mock-ups · Nov 12, 2026

Prototypes from your briefs

Each one is a working page you can click through. None of them is on the site. Each was built by one agent and then checked and fixed by a second at phone, tablet and desktop widths. Your questions sit under each one.

  1. Search · /stories or the nav

    Smart search bar

    Search the whole library as you type. Results come back sorted into stories, the two layover guides and the expat hubs marked coming soon. It works fully from the keyboard. When nothing matches it says "Stuck at the gate?", offers a spelling fix and three random places from the library.

    Open the search bar
    Your decisions
    • Where it lives: the /stories search, a nav search, or both.
    • Keep matching words inside story text? "visa" finds 8 stories, but passing mentions show up too.
    • Show the expat "coming soon" group on every search, or only on expat searches.
    • Keep "Been there yourself? Share your story" on the no-results state.
    • Approve adding title, place, country and date to the search index file.
    • Search from one letter, or start at two.
  2. Onboarding · Home and /signup

    Onboarding widget

    A visitor picks Travel Map or Paw Passport and ticks the lines that are true for them. A dotted world map lights up and "Your profile is N% live" climbs. It ends at a "Claim your map" block that links to the real signup page. Nothing ticked is saved.

    Open the widget
    Your decisions
    • Approve the new copy, which is marked DRAFT: the headline "Start with what you have already survived." and the tick lines.
    • Is "Your profile is N% live" fine on a preview where no profile exists yet?
    • Should the chosen path carry over to /signup?
    • Where it lives, and which site layer it joins to stay under the script cap.
    • Are the two story photos the right ones?
  3. Layout · /stories

    Editorial grid for /stories

    All 57 real stories in one column on a phone, opening into uneven magazine bands on wider screens. Titles and footers line up across each band, however long the titles run. A "Today" tab shows the current live layout for comparison.

    Open the grid
    Your decisions
    • Big cards by position, or by a CMS "pick" flag (only Pompeii has one today).
    • Fix the live text that breaks house style: an em dash in the Pompeii excerpt, two exclamation marks in titles, "centre" in the Southern Africa excerpt.
    • Show excerpts on every card, or only on the lead card.
    • Add a photo credit field to Stories, or keep crediting the contributor.
  4. Browse · /stories and /layovers

    Departures board

    The library sorted like an airport departures board, with three boards: by duration, by objective and by country. Rows marked Boarding open real stories and the live layover guides. Rows marked Later say "Coming soon" and don't link anywhere. It still works with scripts turned off.

    Open the board
    Your decisions
    • The Ko-fi link for the €9 pet guide.
    • Rename "Pet relocation"? No story is about moving a pet. One idea is "Travel with a dog".
    • Which stories go under each objective (a field you would set in the CMS).
    • Should the duration rows list stories (Doha 8 hours, Istanbul 3) as well as guides?
  5. Story pages · mid-story block

    Layover bridge

    A block partway through a story that asks two things about the story's country: add it to your map, and do you have a layover at its airport. On the Sort Sol story it opens the CPH guide's three-line summary. On the São Paulo story it says GRU has no guide yet and invites someone to write one.

    Open the bridge
    Your decisions
    • What "Add to your map" saves: Want to go, Been here, or something new. If it never reaches the map, the button should say "list".
    • How a story picks its airport. Brazil has several, so a per-story airport field is suggested.
    • Show "No layover guide for this airport yet" on every story without a guide, or only where a layover makes sense.
    • Fix "centre" and "harbour" in the live CPH guide.
  6. Pet guide · Paw Passport to Ko-fi

    Pet guide sales flow

    A plan for selling the €9 EU pet travel guide. It follows every place Magnus Waffles appears, through the Ko-fi checkout and back to the Paw Passport, with the exact copy for each step. It includes a clickable phone and desktop mock-up of the guide block and a list of the events to measure.

    Open the flow
    Your decisions
    • Ko-fi details: the product link, the price, whether to show "incl. 25% moms", the page count, the update promise, a refund line.
    • Confirm the guide follows regulation 2026/131 and the date you last checked it.
    • Which stories get the end-of-story link.
    • The byline: the block says "By Nancy Carleton, with Magnus Waffles". The live page doesn't name the author.
    • The page code still has the old 12, 3 and 13 counts for readers without scripts.
  7. Expat · Copenhagen guide

    The Line Counter: Copenhagen paperwork

    A paperwork guide for new arrivals in Copenhagen. Pick EU/EEA or not and it lists every office and deadline in order, each with its official source and a tick box. A visa-date tracker works out your next deadline in your own browser. The dates are marked SAMPLE until a Location Expert checks them.

    Open the guide
    Your decisions
    • The facts come from search excerpts. A Copenhagen Location Expert has to open every source link before the SAMPLE strip comes off.
    • Emergency pet care has no official link. Could a Danish veterinary authority page count as a source?
    • Point "Become a Location Expert" straight at the application section.
    • Keep the tracker in the browser, or make it a member feature with email reminders.
    • The URL, and where the guide sits in the nav.
  8. Motion · Home and story cards

    Two small effects

    Point at the home cover's coordinates and a "View map" badge follows the pointer to the map link. Save a story or read to the end and the magazine's postmark stamps its card. Both run on real stories and come as separate CSS and JS blocks, ready to go into a site layer.

    Open the effects
    Your decisions
    • A badge cursor or a compass cursor (badge recommended).
    • Saving stories needs storage through the Worker and a Save button on each card.
    • Which corner the stamp takes on home cards, where a decorative stamp already sits.
    • Can a story get the read-to-end stamp without being saved first?
    • The stamp's own lettering is about 5 to 9px. It's decorative, but it is under the 12px rule.
  9. Type · whole site

    Fluid type system

    Type sizes that scale smoothly from a 360px phone to a 1920px monitor, with the math shown for each size. A table maps the site's current font settings to the new names. Resize the window and a live bar shows the real sizes and characters per line.

    Open the type system
    Your decisions

    • Shrink section titles from 34–64px to 28–48px, or keep them big.
    • Let story subheads grow to about 42px on desktop, or keep 28px.
    • Confirm the kit's letter spacing (-0.01em) wins over the tighter live values.

    Structured data templates

    A reference page for the hidden data search engines read, with templates for stories, layover guides and the expat Q&A. Each sits next to what the live page sends today. It says plainly which Google rich results no longer exist, and a form fills a template the way Webflow does.

    Founding badge

    A member presses "Make my badge" and the browser draws their founding badge as an image for a Story, a square post or X. There are two designs, the Traveler badge and the Paw Passport stamp. The share step only offers what that browser can actually do, and nothing is posted for the member.

    Dashboard map animation

    The animation player for chapter 02 of the home page, "Your travel dashboard". The map opens, a stamp drops when you tick a trip, and a route connects your places. Your saved places always stay on the map. It plays a stand-in drawing marked STAND-IN until the Fable export is attached. It draws nothing while off screen, and with reduced motion it jumps straight to the end of each step.

    Open the dashboard map
    Your decisions

    • Where the Paw Passport stamp lives: on the guide card in chapter 04, or add a guide link to chapter 02.
