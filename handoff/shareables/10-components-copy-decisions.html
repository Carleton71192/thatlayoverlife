OPEN DECISIONS FOR NANCY
Founder's headline countUN 193 (recommended), TLL classic 195 or Been-style 250. Clean the corrupt "undefined" entry first, whatever the choice.
Final tier namesCarry-On, Checked Bag, Half the Stamps, Centurion, UN-Done are samples. "Half the Stamps" at 50 is not half of 193.
Is Airside self-reported?Or does Escaped need a photo? Self-report is simpler and matches how members already log the map.
Magnus's countStored as 13. Under UN 193 he is 12 plus Kosovo as a bonus. His list has no Denmark, where he lives: add it?
Companion purple#7C5CBF passes on both themes and stays distinct from rose for red-green color blindness in my check, but it always carries stripes and a stroke. Confirm the hex.
Map data migrationToday each country stores lived, visited or layover. The ladder needs airside, escaped, fed, slept, lived. Visited becomes Escaped. Layover is ambiguous: ask members, or default to Escaped so nobody's count drops overnight.
Stamp yearsEvery year on this board is a placeholder. Real cards read years from the member's data.
Parks total"63 US national parks" is commonly cited but not verified against nps.gov. The parks switch shows a sample number until it is.
Legal reviewA short GDPR check on public travel-history profiles before Public goes live.
Link previewsPer-member preview images need a small server route (Webflow Cloud or a CMS sync). Ship downloadable cards first.
