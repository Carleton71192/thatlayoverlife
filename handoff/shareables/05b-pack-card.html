HOW THE PACK WORKS
Add by handle. On My pets, type a friend's Instagram handle and tap Ask them.
Their human says yes. Nothing shows until they approve. Pending requests stay hidden.
On TLL already? The friend shows their own Paw Passport photo and links to it.
Instagram only? Handle plus a photo their human uploaded or approved. Never a scraped or embedded post.
Leaving is one tap. Either human can remove a friend. The card redraws without them.
Counts are real. "{n} friends" counts approved friends only. Nine or more fills the 3×3 grid; fewer shows open slots.
