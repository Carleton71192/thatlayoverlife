HOW THE THREE LEVELS BEHAVE
Off. No public URL exists. The member can still make and download every card.
Unlisted. A random slug like /p/k7f3q9, served with noindex. New link invalidates the old one. Revoke returns to Off.
Public. /p/{handle}, indexable, with a real link preview image.
