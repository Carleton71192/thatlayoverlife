## 3. Tell the editor button

- On a page with the home hero, the button now waits until the hero has cleared the bottom of the screen, then fades up in 200ms (ease-out; opacity only with reduced motion). On first load at 390 it no longer sits on the hero; after a short scroll it appears where it was.
- Keyboard focus brings it back at once, so it is never unreachable.
- `aria-label="Tell the editor"` is set on the button. Its visible words were already in the markup at font-size 0 on phones; the label makes the name explicit.
- At 1440 the hero ends above the button's spot, so nothing changes there.

## 4. /contact website field

`website-url` is the relay form's spam trap: it is pushed off screen, skipped by Tab (tabindex -1), and the form refuses to send when it is filled. A visible label would invite people to fill it and their message would be silently blocked. It now has `aria-label="Leave this field empty"` and `aria-hidden="true"`, so audit tools see a named field and screen readers skip it. Also noticed: all four inputs on /contact share `id="field"`, so the labels' `for` links are ambiguous; worth a separate fix.

## 5. Proposal: Maldives Teal on light backgrounds (decision needed)

Ratios: #00C9C8 on Cream 1.8:1, on Paper 2.0:1, on Midnight 9.3:1. Deep teal #067A79 on Cream 4.6:1, on Paper 4.9:1, white text on #067A79 5.2:1.

- **Option A (recommended): bright teal stays on dark.** #00C9C8 for text, icons and the map only on Midnight, Ink and dark photo gradients. On Paper and Cream, labels and small accents use Deep Teal #067A79, links are Ink with a Sunset Rose underline, and teal buttons are #067A79 with white text. This is what the story and account pages already do.
- **Option B: same as A, named as a token.** "Maldives Teal" keeps #00C9C8; "Maldives Teal Deep" #067A79 is its light-surface form in the kit, so nobody picks the bright one on Cream again.
- **Not viable:** bright teal on light even at display sizes. 1.8:1 fails the 3:1 floor for large text and for non-text marks.

If you pick A or B, the follow-up changes are small: the contributor profile stat label (head code line 17, after a browser check) and the home atlas hover underline (#00C9C8 on Paper, decorative but faint) move to #067A79.
