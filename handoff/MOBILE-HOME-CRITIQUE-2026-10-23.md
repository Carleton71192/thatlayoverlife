# Home on a phone: critique and plan (23 October 2026)

Nancy, 23 Oct, with a 390px screenshot of the live home hero: "The mobile design aesthetic is a mess. Needs a designer's UX touch."

## What is wrong on this one screen

1. Two button languages in the header: a rounded "Nancy" pill next to a square, letter-spaced MENU box. Pick one shape.
2. The eyebrow "TRAVEL STORIES BY PEOPLE WHO WERE THERE" is bright teal on a mid-blue photo (fails contrast; teal is for dark grounds only) and wraps to two lines with very wide tracking. The 3.4 design has no eyebrow here.
3. The headline breaks as "Find your / next / somewhere." The 3.4 design sets it with text-wrap balance at 52px+ and anchors it to the bottom of the photo.
4. The lede sits in a half-width column, leaving the right half empty over a busy photo.
5. About 120px of dead space between the lede and the buttons.
6. Square buttons at about 58 percent width, left aligned: neither full width (phone pattern) nor content width. The 3.4 phone design makes them full width.
7. "FREE, FOREVER. NO CARD. NO PAYWALL." is covered by the floating "Tell the editor" pill.
8. The coordinates line "12.17° N 68.99° W" is clipped at the bottom right.
9. The photo is cropped so the boat is cut off at the right edge, and the hero is a rounded "sheet" with a dark line under the header, a shape used nowhere else.

## What the approved design (prototype 3.4) does instead

Full-bleed photo, headline at the bottom of the photo, lede and the two buttons in one block under it, buttons full width on phones, the FREE FOREVER line in teal on the dark scrim, the Tell the editor pill pinned bottom-left and hidden while the cookie banner shows.
