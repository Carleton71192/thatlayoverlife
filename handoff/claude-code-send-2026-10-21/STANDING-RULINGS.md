# TLL standing rulings (Nancy)

- **Voice:** Lonely Planet meets Klarna meets **the National Park Service sign** — quirky, dry, funny. NOT "campfire." Kit signatures: dry parenthetical aside, "I know how this sounds" preempt, named characters with one defining detail, signed sign-off, sensory anchors. No "honestly/look/anyway" tics.
- **Voice update (Nancy 2026-10-20, sharpens the Voice line above):** write like a frequent traveler and expat talking to their own kind. Someone who has slept on an airport floor, rebooked a cancelled flight at the desk, packs a Ryanair-size backpack and goes, loves bougie lounge wine AND the best meal of their life in someone's small kitchen. Warm, playful, insider details (23 kg of snacks from home, translating spices in the grocery aisle, a residence card and opinions about it, the photo from the gate, a heated-blanket Samoyed at 6 a.m.).
  - **Short, punchy, fun.** One idea per line. Fragments are fine. Cut every adjective that is not doing a job; no stacked descriptions. If a line can lose words and keep its meaning, cut them.
  - Casual words she uses are on-voice: "cool kids", "inspo", "bestie", "side quests", "adventure".
  - Never preachy, never brochure. Inclusive: no assumptions about where people move (no "Schengen days" in general expat copy; some people move to Africa).
  - Counts copy: "Counted from your map" (your, not the). Do not say "never typed" in UI copy (Nancy 2026-10-20); COUNT-RULES.md still governs the source.
