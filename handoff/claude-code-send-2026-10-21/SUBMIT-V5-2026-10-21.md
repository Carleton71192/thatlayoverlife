## Never lose live content
- Keep the form name and every field name below. Existing submissions, CMS items and Make/Zapier hooks depend on them.
- Restyle in place. Do not delete or rename a field; where v5 hides one, keep it as a hidden input with its current value.
- Export Stories CSV + form submissions before and after. Any count drop = stop.

## Layout
- Mode picker: **Write it here** / **Link to it** (two cards).
- One Paper panel, sticky sub nav TRIP · STORY · PHOTOS · EXTRAS · BYLINE · SEND (same pattern as /edit-profile). Dot = section's rules pass.
- Right rail (desktop, sticky): **The rules** checklist (Ink panel), progress bar, Send button, live story card preview. Phone: rail hides, fixed bottom bar "n of 8 rules · Next: …" + Send.
- Send stays disabled until every rule passes; tapping it scrolls to the first miss.
- After send: "It's live." + See your story / Write another. Email copy to writer. No "48 hours" anywhere on this path.
