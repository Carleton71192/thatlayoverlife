WHAT THAT LAYOVER LIFE IS
That Layover Life is a travel storytelling platform and community engine for repeat travelers, expats, and anyone who can turn a six-hour connection into a story worth telling. Contributors write, an editor in Copenhagen reads every word, and every submission gets a human review within 48 hours with the byline kept. Free to read, no paywall on the archive.
