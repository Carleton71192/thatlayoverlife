## v2.2 updates, 29 October 2026

Decisions Nancy made or approved on 29 October 2026 while testers worked through the live site. Each one is a standing rule from today. Where an older line above disagrees, it is tagged [replaced 29 Oct 2026] and this block wins.

1. **Hover and active states:** On light backgrounds (Paper, Cream, white cards) hover, active and current-page states use dark rose #C8325B: ink text, a #C8325B border or underline. Never bright teal #00C9C8 on a light background; it is too light. The member bar (OVERVIEW, PROFILE, MAP, PETS, CONTRIBUTIONS, SETTINGS) marks the current page with a #C8325B underline and lines up with the page column, not the window edge. Maldives Teal stays the hover color on Midnight.
2. **Photos:** Every photo on the site is a member's own photo or art. Never ads, never stock. A photo is credited to its writer and links to the story it came from. Captions name the place of a published story, never where anyone is now.
3. **Link-out share images:** When a writer links a story published elsewhere, the article's share image is offered only when it is a real photo: JPEG, PNG or WebP, at least 600 x 315, not banner or strip shaped, and not from an ad, logo, icon or placeholder address. It is used only while the writer keeps "Use this photo from the article" ticked.
4. **Cover photo treatment:** The cover photo has to read. Use a light scrim: a soft shade behind the text side and a short fade at the top and bottom, plus a soft shadow on the headline. No heavy dark overlay across the whole photo.
5. **Place filters:** "All places" first, then the continents in alphabetical order: Africa, Americas, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, Middle East, Oceania. Every region in the Stories region field gets a chip, even before it has a story.
6. **People and pets:** People and pets are shown apart. Travelers lists people; pets sit in their own "Travel companions" block with a link to The Paw Passport. Search on Travelers reads "Name, city or country".
7. **Forms:** Mark what is needed: a rose "Required" chip on required fields, an "Optional" chip on optional blocks, "Opt in" on anything a member chooses to join (for example the expat desks). Long forms are collapsible blocks, and save state shows in a bar at the bottom of the screen.
8. **Moderation stance (draft, pending Nancy's legal review):** Flag harmful content rather than edit or pre-approve. Platform line: "We don't rewrite your story. A human reviews it within 48 hours and only flags what breaks the charter." Until the legal review is done, the older "lightly edited" and "reviewed first" lines stay live and are tagged [replaced 29 Oct 2026, draft].
9. **Language and translation:** Platform copy is American English. Place names keep their own spelling and accents ("São Paulo" with the tilde). A story is published in the language its writer chose and the original is never altered. Any translation is labeled, never auto-applied, and "Show the original" is always one tap away.
10. **Banned words (union, nothing removed):** Never: em dashes; "ships" in any form; ledger; "No filler"; "Truth over polish"; "real names"; filed; the shelf; counted by hand; "Currently in". The library is "the library", never "the shelf".
11. **Type and color reminder:** Playfair Display 800 headlines, IBM Plex Sans Condensed body, JetBrains Mono eyebrows and labels, Syne only in the wordmark. Ink text on rose chips. Links on light backgrounds are Deep Teal #067A79.
