A free place to tell the story of a trip, keep a map of everywhere you have been, and find someone who lives where you are headed. I'm Nancy. I read everything that comes in, supervised by a 35-kilogram Samoyed.

Why this exists.

It started in an airport lounge, where the wine was doing what lounge wine does: making the next trip look like an excellent idea.

I did not want a blog that needs feeding. I wanted a space that belongs to the people using it: log a trip, share it, skip the politics. So I built that, and left the door open.

I swear by the Mark Twain line about travel:

"Travel is fatal to prejudice, bigotry, and narrow-mindedness, and many of our people need it sorely on these accounts."

MARK TWAIN · THE INNOCENTS ABROAD · 1869

He wrote that more than 150 years ago and it has not needed an update.

RULE 08 IN FULL · DRAFT, NANCY TO APPROVE
Paid work, in plain view.
TLL is also the portfolio for The Brand Collective, Nancy's studio. Here is exactly where the money goes and where it stops.
A
Who pays whom
Tourism boards, airlines and hotels hire The Brand Collective. Invoices come from the studio. Nobody buys space on TLL.
B
What they get
Creator casting, stopover content, a campaign. Delivered to them, for their channels, under their name.
C
What they never get
Approval of a TLL story. A kinder verdict. A place in the Layover Awards. A higher spot in any directory. Those are not for sale at any price (we checked, the price is no).
D
When paid work touches a story
The word Reklame sits at the top, before the headline. The client is named, and so is what they covered: the flight, the room, the fee. The story's Disclosure line says the same thing.
E
The kill switch
If a client asks to change a verdict, the story runs as written or it doesn't run. The invoice stands either way.
F
The public list
Every client whose work has touched TLL is listed here with the year.
