## Findings

### 🟡 High

1. **Two sitemap URLs return 404.** `/what-were-good-at`, `/da/det-er-vi-gode-til`. Why it matters: Google is sent to dead ends on the services section, the one section a prospect needs. Fix: pack 02. Tier: developer. Effort: Quick (option B) or Medium (option A). Evidence: Ahrefs Site Audit 20 Aug and 27 Aug 2026, "4XX page in sitemap: 2"; Notion task 3ce9feee open since 1 Sep.
2. **Address consistency not yet confirmed live.** Google's indexed copy of `/about/contact` still reads "Højbro Plads 10, 1200 Copenhagen K". Notion records the site fix as done on 21 Sep. Either the snippet is stale or the static cache still serves the old page. Why it matters: name, address and phone must match the Business Profile (Bredgade 6, 1260 København K) or local ranking is suppressed. Fix: confirm live text, clear cache, then ship the schema in pack 01. Tier: developer. Effort: Quick. Evidence: Google search snippet, 28 Sep 2026; Asger Kelstrup email signature 30 Jul 2026 "Bredgade 6, 1260 Copenhagen K".
3. **Consent banner is Danish on English pages and full-screen on phones.** Why it matters: an English-speaking prospect on a phone sees Danish cookie text and no headline. Fix: pack 03. Tier: needs a person (Cookie Information login) plus one template line. Effort: Quick. Evidence: layout source recorded 10 Jul 2026, `data-culture="DA"` static.
4. **Duplicate homepage at `/42tax`.** Indexed with the homepage's old title. Why it matters: two copies of the most important page split its authority. Fix: pack 05, part 2. Tier: developer. Effort: Quick. Evidence: Google result for "42TAX Bredgade 6", 28 Sep 2026.
