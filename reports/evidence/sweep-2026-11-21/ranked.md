## How to read it

- Duplicates across lenses are merged into one row; the source ids are listed in the first column.
- KNOWN items (already in handoff/NANCY-WHEN-HOME.md or an earlier release note) are in their own list at the end,
  with any new detail the sweep added.
- Ranking is user impact over effort, inside each class. Class A comes first.
  - **A**: safe to fix now in the core layers or repo code on a branch. No Webflow write except a core release.
  - **B**: needs a Webflow page-code write or a Designer change. We prepare the exact before and after text.
  - **C**: needs Nancy's decision. We prepare a one-line question with a recommendation.
- Effort: XS under 30 minutes, S under half a day, M a day or more (including test and release).
- Severity is the lens severity, raised or lowered only where the merge changed the picture (noted).

Counts: 21 A, 23 B, 12 C, 10 KNOWN, 1 watch item.
