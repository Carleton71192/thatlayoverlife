# Release 16.67.0, regression check: real test site against real www (17 November 2026)

Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES. Nothing found that should keep 16.67.0 off www.

Hosts: that-layover-life.webflow.io (core 16.67.0, pin a254306f, published 17 Nov 10:29:16 UTC) against www.thatlayover.life (core 16.66.0, pin 3df16988). Every test load ran core a254306f and every www load ran 3df16988 (column `core` in loads.csv).

## Method
- harness/r.cjs (one Chromium at a time, isMobile at phone widths), harness/an.py, harness/chain.sh. 660 loads in 17 sets (11:29 to 12:40 UTC, then 16:36 to 17:10 UTC after a machine restart cut the run; finished loads were skipped on the restart).
- The first navigation of each load is one real GET of the real host; later navigations of a flow (reload, back, forward, the other page) are served this checker's plain-GET copies. jsDelivr files of our repo come from `git show <pin>:<path>` (byte equal to jsDelivr). Webflow images, video and fonts come from the local caches only; a miss is aborted (same on both hosts). Memberstack is served patched (signed out) or as a signed-in stand-in with no name and a made-up address. Every non-GET was aborted (2,664 in all, none sent). Ads and analytics were answered with an empty body.
- CLS counts every layout-shift entry, including those Chromium marks hadRecentInput under isMobile with no input sent. The input-excluded figure is in its own column. "Finished page equals www" compares the end box list of each test load with the www loads of the same cell.
- Webflow hosts: about 47 MB (documents only; the counter read 74.7 MB because loads before 12:15 UTC counted documents unpacked). No image, video or font was taken from Webflow hosts.

## Notes
A. /stories under slow 4G has a two-state race on both hosts: at the web-font swap (about 10 to 11 s) the grid and the bar above it are pushed out of view and come back about half a second later. At 390 test hits this state in 4 of 11 loads (0.265 to 0.268) and www in 1 of 11 (0.323); at 1440 4 of 11 on both (test 0.14 to 0.22, www 0.27 to 0.29); at 360 test 1 of 3 (0.233), www 2 of 3 (0.30). Medians are lower on test at every width (390 0.014 against 0.029, 1440 0.084 against 0.101, 360 0.016 against 0.299). Same entries on both hosts; not caused by the new layers, but at 390 the odds moved the wrong way.
B. When the /stories lead script (tll-lib-lead-v1) and the chips script (tll-lib-chips-v1) both throw, test jumps once at about 0.9 s (390 and 360: 0.368 against 0.013 on www, in all 8 loads where both scripts throw (7 at 390, 1 at 360); 1440, all four footer scripts throwing: 0.256 against 0.030, where the end boxes differ from www by a card photo that is not in the local cache). Either script alone, or any pair without both of them, stays at or below www. Cause: the early state sets the lead card and the date order before the first paint and hands the grid back in the HTML order at DOMContentLoaded, right before the page code runs; either page script puts the order back, so only a failure of both shows the HTML order after the first paint. A double failure of inline page code; not a reason to hold the release.
C. Back to /account at 1440 (signed out) after scrolling to the bottom: test lands 77 px above the wanted spot in 4 of 4 loads (both back steps); www lands right in 7 of 8 back steps. CLS on these steps is lower on test (0.37 against 0.52).
D. /submit at 390 with the site head parity script throwing: test 0.141 against 0.000 (one load). The script is inline in the head and has not been seen failing.
