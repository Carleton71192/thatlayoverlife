# /edit-profile: simplification proposal

DESIGN stage, read-only, 12 October 2026. Webflow page 6a0df371cd5b09ac504421ae, title "Edit your profile · That Layover Life", robots noindex,follow, last updated 2026-10-12T12:39Z. Nothing in this document has been applied. Winning angle: Prototype-first (two of three judge votes), with the grafts the judges flagged from the other two angles folded in.

Design read: Operate mode, a signed-in member completing a task. Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 4, MOTION_INTENSITY 3, VISUAL_DENSITY 5. Brand kit wins on type and color (Playfair Display 800 display, IBM Plex Sans Condensed body, JetBrains Mono eyebrow and legends; Ink, Paper, Line, Rose on the one primary action, Deep Maldives teal as the system color for legends, on-states and the focus halo).

## 1. Six lines for Nancy

1. **What the page is for.** It is where a signed-in Traveler writes the public version of themselves: the fields the Travelers list and the public profile show, which directories they appear in, which links are shown, and how people can reach them.
2. **What is wrong today.** Three editors with three Save buttons and three data stores sit on one page. The first screen is a dark count card and an injected logging card before any profile field, so the page reads as a map tool. Two link stacks ask for the same accounts into different fields. Countries can be typed in three places next to a card that says the map owns them. Nothing is marked required, so a new member meets 23 blank boxes, 3 switches, 7 link rows and a contact block with no sense of what matters. The switches are 38x22px, examples live only in placeholders, every text input shares one id, three focus rings compete, and the directory editor is set in Inter with 12px radii, all off the v9 spec.
3. **What changes.** The page is rebuilt as the single-column editor of prototype §25, with the Account v3 "Edit profile" tab filling in what §25 leaves undrawn: THE BASICS first with exactly two required fields, OPEN TO as chips with the newsletter as one plain sentence, the typed numbers behind a disclosure with the Countries box gone, the map card after the form, "Switch on what fits." with 52x30 switches and one opt-in link stack, HOW PEOPLE REACH YOU, designations as three rows, and one sticky Save profile bar that saves the directory switches and then the Memberstack form. The injected logging card and the eight always-on URL fields are hidden. v9 type and tokens, 4px radius, one focus ring, no scroll fade-in, and every state designed (loading skeleton, empty-profile nudge, inline errors, saving, saved, partial save, failed, signed out, unsaved changes on exit).
4. **What stays.** Every Memberstack block, binding, input name and id; every data-tll-edit hook and the hidden Interest Tags list; the three sign-in gates; every link target and mailto subject; the press-trip consent sentence character for character; the page title and noindex. Everything is wrap, hide, reorder, relabel: no element is removed, renamed or re-bound.
5. **Required inputs.** Before: 0 marked on the profile form (so nothing tells a member what matters), plus two conditional ones in the side editors. After: 2, Name and The place you know best.
6. **What only you can decide.** Where Log a place lives after your rename; which link stack is canonical and whether old values migrate; whether typed country counts are cleared; the two required fields; one Save in sequence or two labeled saves; when the member-to-CMS webhook goes on; whether the 48h promise stays; an avatar block; and the two Memberstack custom fields (youtube-url, continents-marathoned) that still need creating by hand.

## 2. The first screen, top to bottom

**Signed-in member, 1280 wide, before any scroll.** The nav. A mono Rose eyebrow, YOUR PROFILE · EDIT MODE. The Playfair 800 H1 "Edit your profile." with "profile" in Rose italic (unchanged). One lede sentence: "This is the public version of you. Nothing changes until you hit Save profile." When both Name and The place you know best are blank after Memberstack has prefilled, a teal mono line under the lede: "Two fields put you on the Travelers list: your name and the place you know best." Then the white THE BASICS card: the legend, the help line "Who you are, and where you know.", the Name field with its REQUIRED tag, The place you know best with its REQUIRED tag and the help line "A city or a country. Where you know, never where you are.", and the top of the Fun title field. Pinned to the bottom edge, the Paper save bar: the status line on the left, "Cancel and go back" as a ghost link, and the Rose "Save profile" button on the right. No dark card, no injected logging card, no count, and nothing fades in except one 200ms opacity on the header.

**Phone, 390 wide.** Eyebrow, H1, lede, the nudge when it applies, then THE BASICS with Name and the start of The place you know best; the bar pinned with a 44px button and the status line wrapped above it. 16px side gutters, no horizontal scroll.

**Signed out, any width.** Eyebrow PROFILE EDITOR, H2 "Sign in to tell us who you are.", lede "Editing your profile is a Travelers thing. Free to join, free forever.", the Log in and Or join the Travelers buttons. While Memberstack is still answering, a skeleton (a 48px bar where the H1 goes and one white card outline with three gray lines) instead of a blank Paper page.

**The three readers.** A first-time reader gets "this is where I edit my profile, and nothing changes until I save" from the eyebrow, H1 and lede. A would-be contributor sees that two fields get them on the Travelers list. An advertiser or editor sees the same editor the Travelers list is built from and, one scroll down, OPEN TO and the directory switches that decide what they get to see.

**Layout.** One column, max-width 760px, centered, 64px top padding on desktop (48px under 760px), the section background Paper #FDF9F3 kept, 96px bottom padding so nothing hides under the bar. Cards: #fff, 1px Line #E0DACE, 4px radius, 22px padding, 22px gaps. Legends JetBrains Mono 11px 700, tracking 2px, #067A79. Labels IBM Plex Sans Condensed 14px 700 Ink above every control; help 14px #5B564F below the label; inputs 16px Ink on Paper, 1.5px #C9C3B8 border, 4px radius, 48px min height. Chips 16px radius; switches 52x30 pills. Shape lock: 4px on cards, inputs and buttons; 16px on chips; pill only on switches. Accent lock: Rose #D63859 on Save profile only (plus the H1 italic and the eyebrow, which are brand fixtures); teal is the system color for legends, on-states, the REQUIRED tag and the focus halo; nothing orange. Focus: 3px Ink outline, 2px offset, 6px teal halo, on every control. Mono labels on the whole page: the eyebrow plus five card legends (THE BASICS, OPEN TO, YOUR MAP, YOUR LINKS, HOW PEOPLE REACH YOU); the directory and designations blocks get Playfair headlines with no label above them, so the eyebrow-plus-headline pair appears once, at the top.

**Visual order inside div[data-ms-content=members].** 1 header, 2 the form (THE BASICS, the Your numbers disclosure, OPEN TO), 3 the map card, 4 the directory block (Switch on what fits., three switch rows that unlock their questions, the rule line, YOUR LINKS, HOW PEOPLE REACH YOU), 5 designations as three rows with the consent sentence under the Press row, 6 the account row, 7 the save bar. All of it by CSS order and script-inserted wrappers; the DOM order of every Memberstack block is untouched.
