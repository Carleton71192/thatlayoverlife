## How it gets built

- **Same method as homepage 3.0, Stories 3.0, Map 3.0 and Profile 3.0.** New HTML embeds and bound blocks go in, plus one namespaced style block per page: `c3-` on the Countries Template and `d3-` on /destinations. They use the tll-system.css tokens, copied in the same way home3.css copied them. tll-system.css is not loaded as a file.
- **Old parts are hidden, never deleted.** Every element ID, id attribute (#tll-filed, #tll-filed-link, #tllcf-layovers, #tllcf-races, #tllcf-diary, #atlas-all), data-* attribute, CMS binding and collection list stays. The typed /destinations story cards and the guide chips are hidden, not removed.
- **Content stays bound to the CMS.** Nothing a traveler wrote is typed into an embed. The prototype's Bulgaria answers, quote, wire item and counts are sample data and are not copied (open point 15).
- **CMS fields.** The Countries collection has 52 custom fields plus Name and Slug, so 8 of Webflow's 60 are free. The default build needs no new field: everything comes from existing Countries fields, the referenced story (Story Ref) and its fields, the Directory and Resources lists already on the page, and the Stories collection. Stories is at the 60-field cap, so nothing can be added there. Any new field is an open point (points 1 and 5).
- **Counting rule.** Total = Lived + Stayed for the member's own (non-pet) travelers, layovers counted apart, out of 249. Nothing on these pages prints a member total. The one place a member's map is read (row C3, "On your map") shows that country's own depth, never a count. Story and country counts on /destinations are computed from the rendered collection lists at run time. No count is typed.
- **Map codes.** Wherever a country page or the atlas links into the map, ISO2 is turned into the map's numeric id by a small table in the c3 script, with Kosovo = 900, Northern Cyprus = 901 and Somaliland = 902 (S32). A raw "900" is never printed.
- **tllStates is written only by the map.** "Been here" and "Want to go" keep writing member JSON tllPlaces exactly as today (D03). The country page may read tllStates to show "On your map: Stayed", and nothing more.
- **Motion (Emil rules):**
  - Hover and press only. Story cards, atlas tiles, the two toggle pills and the CTA buttons get a 150ms ease-out border or color change and scale(.97) on press, transitions on named properties only (no `transition: all`).
  - The live `.tlldx-card:hover` and `.tllcx-filed-card:hover` outline jumps are replaced by the same color change.
  - The prototype has no other motion on these screens; none is added. No count-up, no scroll reveal.
  - Hover effects only under `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
  - With prefers-reduced-motion, transitions are off; the hero photo never moves.
- **Draft first.** Each page is built on a draft duplicate or staged unpublished, and the live pages change only after Nancy says yes.
- **Not touched:**
  - TLL Nav v1, TLL Site Footer, the feedback button.
  - Memberstack data-ms-* blocks: these two pages have none. The tllPlaces script reads the member through `$memberstackDom` and is kept as it is.
  - CMS items and slugs (copy fixes in items are open points, not edits).
  - The site head and footer code and the site scripts.
  - The /countries forward to /destinations (B40) and the legacy /destinations/{slug} redirect.

## SEO, AEO and accessibility

- **Country pages are answer pages.**
  - Current: title "{Name} · Country Guide", description = Verdict, OG copied from SEO, canonical /countries/{slug}. Good, kept.
  - Each section answers one question under its own h2 (row C13). The direct answer leads: h1, then the Verdict in one or two sentences, then the travelers' quick answers.
  - Quick answers are the traveler's own words from the story's Quick answers field, attributed and dated, with a link to the story. Nothing is written for a country that has no story.
  - Sources are listed with their own checked or reviewed dates (row C12), as audit-2026-10-09 asks. The page never implies official authority: travel advice links out to Canada and the UK FCDO.
- **Structured data, country template.**
  - Today: Country (#country, description = Verdict, address.addressCountry = ISO2) and BreadcrumbList Home > Countries (/countries) > {Name}. Crumb 2 points at /countries, which now forwards to /destinations.
  - Proposal: crumb 2 becomes "Destinations", https://www.thatlayover.life/destinations. Add a WebPage node (#webpage, about = #country, dateModified = Data checked when set, citation = the Advisory URL and FCDO URL when set, isPartOf #website).
  - FAQPage: written by the c3 script only when the quick answers block renders, with exactly the questions and answers visible on the page, each answer citing the story URL. No FAQPage on pages without a story. Google shows FAQ rich results only for a small set of sites now, so the value is in answer engines reading clean Q&A, not in a SERP feature.
  - Validate with the Rich Results Test and Schema.org validator on Bulgaria, Ghana and Chad after the draft build.
- **Structured data, /destinations.**
  - Today: CollectionPage with a typed description ("Six countries have a page so far: Bulgaria, Chad, Denmark, Ghana, Japan, Montenegro. … 189 to go.") and a typed ItemList (numberOfItems 6, Chad included though it has no story).
  - Proposal: CollectionPage description without numbers ("Stories by country from TLL travelers, and a page for every country with checked facts and links to official travel advice."), the typed ItemList removed from page settings, and an ItemList of the countries with a story written by the d3 script from the rendered tiles. BreadcrumbList stays.
