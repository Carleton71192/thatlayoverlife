Review notes

  1. No login wall. Logged-out visitors see the form. The Memberstack anonymous/members blocks move to the end as "Sign in to send" (frame E). That move is yours in the Designer, because data-ms blocks are yours. The existing autosave (tllStoryDraft) keeps the draft through sign-in.
  2. Monthly prompt on /submit: same Prompts collection item as the home page (theme and close date bound). Hidden when no prompt is open.
  3. "Link a story you already published" is an equal first choice. Its five fields stay as they are live today; this artboard does not redesign them.
  4. Prompt tag: arriving from a prompt button (?prompt=slug) ticks this box; it fills a new hidden "prompt" input in the story form (a plain Webflow form field, not Memberstack), so you can see which submissions answer the prompt. Unticking it files the story as a normal submission.
  5. Reading time is computed from the word count (230 words a minute), and the region from the country. The writer never types either, and both stay editable by you.
  6. Photo after sign-in? If the Memberstack Google sign-in reloads the page, a chosen file cannot survive the reload. Then the photo field moves to after sign-in (open question from the submit change log).
  7. The disclosure question is required, with no default. A pre-ticked "No" would let a hosted trip slip through unlabeled. That makes six required inputs, still inside the prompt’s seven. Sponsored and hosted labels and rel="sponsored" keep working as they do now.
  8. More options holds every other field, grouped and collapsed by default. Required flags come off excerpt, region, reader question, sign-off and hashtags (Designer step, change log row 4). Webflow’s hidden junk fields go (row 8).
  9. Filing line shows the computed region and reading time, so the writer sees what the site did without explaining the machinery.
  10. Google first, then email. This is the existing Memberstack signup form (Google button, email, password, 16+ box), restyled per the account-auth 3.0 proposal and not rewired. The button label stays whatever the live Memberstack form says; "Get In →" is shown because it is the protected line.
  11. On-voice states replace Webflow’s "Thank you! Your submission has been received!" and "Oops! Something went wrong". Copy follows Kit v2.1: "reviewed within 48 hours", never "published within".
  12. Desktop step rail lets a writer jump back to any finished step. Focus moves to each step’s heading on Continue; errors list at the top and link to the field.
  13. "What we want" panel: a one-screen brief, with three live stories as examples (titles are live Stories items). It replaces the long helper copy above the form.
