# TLL usability test: a 20-minute script for three people (Phase 3)

For Nancy to run with three people who fit the ICP: repeat travelers who fill passports. On their own phones, not yours. One person at a time. No help unless they are stuck for a full minute.

## Before you start (2 minutes)

Say this, more or less:

"I am testing the site, not you. Nothing you do is wrong. Please think out loud: say what you are looking for, what you expect to happen, and what surprises you. If you get stuck, that is useful, so tell me. I will mostly stay quiet."

Then:
- Have them open www.thatlayover.life on their phone, signed out, in their usual browser.
- Start a timer you can see. Note the time at the start of each task.
- Do not point at the screen. If they ask "where is X?", answer "where would you look?"

## The four tasks (about 4 minutes each)

Read each task aloud, once. Note the start time, the finish time, every place they hesitate for more than 5 seconds, and anything they say.

**Task 1.** "Find a story about somewhere you have been."
- Done when: a story page is open and they say it is about a place they have been (or that no story matches).
- Watch for: do they use search, the country list, the home page, or scroll? Do they find the search box? Do they notice the series labels or filters?

**Task 2.** "Start your own map and add three countries."
- Done when: three countries are saved on their map.
- Watch for: where do they look for "join"? Do they understand "Free. No card. 16+."? After sign-up, do they find the map on their own? Do the Lived / Stayed / Layover marks make sense without explanation? Do they see the welcome steps (if on)?
- If they will not create an account, note that and ask what held them back, then move on.

**Task 3.** "Find out how to share a story."
- Done when: they are on /submit and can say in one sentence what a story is here.
- Watch for: do they go through the menu, a story page, or the account page? Do they read "What a story looks like here"? Can they say what we would send back?

**Task 4.** "Find the dog travel guide and how to buy it."
- Done when: they are on the pet page and can point at where buying happens (or say it is not for sale yet).
- Watch for: do they find the pet page from the home page or the menu? Does the page say clearly whether the guide is ready?

## After the tasks (4 minutes)

Ask, in this order, and write the answers down word for word where you can:
1. "In one sentence, what is this site for?"
2. "Who do you think it is for?"
3. "What would make you come back next month?"
4. "Was anything confusing or annoying?"
5. "Anything you expected to find and did not?"

Thank them. Do not explain the site to them until after the questions.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Find a story | | | | |

| 3 How to share a story | | | | |

Targets: task 2 under 2 minutes from tapping Join to the third country saved; every task finished without help by at least two of the three people.

## Rules for the session

- Three people, one at a time, on their own phones.
- No leading questions ("Did you like the big button?"). Ask "what did you notice?" instead.
- Count hesitations, not opinions. Where two of three people stall in the same spot, that is the finding.
- Write the notes within an hour of each session, while it is fresh.
