# From the Pros: invite kit (draft for Nancy, 31 Oct 2026)

Everything here is a draft in your voice for you to change. Nothing is sent.

## How to add a pro (2 minutes)
1. They join like any member (free), so they have a profile.
2. Webflow CMS > Pros > New: Contributor (their profile), Pursuit (their hub), Link name (their name or brand, never keywords), Their site (https), Pro line (their words, 40 characters), Paid or hosted (on if money or a hosted trip is involved), Invited on. Leave Trusted off.
3. Publish the Pro item. Their profile shows a PRO badge, their stories show "From the Pros" and an author box with one link, and they appear on their pursuit hub.
4. Once you have read and approved their first piece, switch Trusted on (the link stops carrying nofollow).

## Rules (from the playbook)
- The story earns the link: first-hand, original, same editorial check as any story.
- One link, on their name or brand.
- Money either way, or a hosted trip: rel="sponsored" and the Reklame label on the story.
- Never require a link back. Offer the badge below; they use it only if they like.
- New pros start like members: links untrusted until you have checked the first piece.

## Invite email (draft)
Subject: Would you write one piece for That Layover Life?

Hi <first name>,

I run That Layover Life, a travel magazine written by the people who went: real places, specific trips, no paywall. I have followed your <work: races, library photos, labyrinths> for a while, and I would love one first-hand piece from you for our <pursuit> hub.

What you get: your byline, a Pro badge on your profile, one link to your site in the author box, a place in the <pursuit> hub, and a share from our Instagram. What it is not: there is no fee either way, and you never need to link back to us.

If that sounds good, reply and I will send the two-minute sign-up. Any length works; one trip, told your way.

Nancy
