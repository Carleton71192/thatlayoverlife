The rules (from the 10 October design)

  1. Invite, then accept. Nothing is shared before a yes, and either side can leave at any time.
  2. A partner never sees your whole map by default; you share trip by trip or country by country.
  3. Both owners can edit a shared pet. Nobody can edit another person's countries.
  4. No accounts for children: a child appears only as a named traveler on a parent's own map.
  5. Counts stay honest: a shared pet counts on its own Paw Passport, never toward a person's total.
