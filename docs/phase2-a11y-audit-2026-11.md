### 1.2 Moderate and minor, repeated across pages

- `aria-allowed-role` (minor, every page): the v9 chrome script in the site head sets `role="banner"` on `<nav class="tll-nav-v1">`; banner is not allowed on a nav.
- `heading-order` (moderate, 16 of the 22 fixtures, 31 of the 44 runs; it does not fire on the three story fixtures, /edit-profile, /stories or /the-paw-passport, and on /account only at 1440): the footer component's `<h4>Navigate</h4>` follows an h2 or h3 column; on /contact and /support the page jumps from h1 to h3 (`.tll-contact-door-title`, `.tll-support-card-title`); on /travelers the eyebrow "Find your people" is an `<h6>` placed before the h1 (`checks.headings` in `axe-travelers-390.json` begins "6 Find your people" then "1 Where the Travelers live."; the 29 Oct fixture and the 13 Oct export `pages/travelers.html` both carry `<h6>Find your people</h6>`), a Designer setting.
- `landmark-one-main` and `region` (moderate): no `<main>` on home (11 nodes outside landmarks, the whole cover), the Stories template (35, 39 and 42 nodes on the three story fixtures), /account, /edit-profile, /login, /signup, /destinations (13 nodes). The skip link on those pages points at `#tll-main`, which exists but is a div.
- `page-has-heading-one` (moderate): /account (390) and /edit-profile show no heading at all above the footer h4s to a signed-out visitor (`checks.headings` in `axe-account-390.json` and both `axe-edit-profile-*.json` list only Navigate, Discover, Contribute, TLL, Guides, Work with us); /my-pets and /share-photos show an h2 sign-in prompt ("Sign in to register your bestie.", "Sign in to drop your photos.") and no h1.
- `empty-heading` (minor): one `<h2 class="tll-lib-title"></h2>` is empty on /stories. In the 29 Oct tree it is the Designer's collection item (`a.tll-lib-card[data-tll-card=cms]`, the card whose image is `placeholder.jpg`) with its bound fields captured empty, so this is most likely a fixture artifact rather than a live story with no name; the heading is styled by the stories head and footer and the site head, and `tll-stories-noimg-v1.js` does not render or touch it. Check one live card.
- Needs review (axe `incomplete`): 1 to 107 `color-contrast` nodes per page where text sits over a photo or a gradient and axe cannot read the background: the cover kicker, chip and h1 on home, the stamp SVG text on story pages, the page-hero photo captions (`.tll-ph-cap`) on about and for-expats, the hero eyebrow and h1 on contact, support and stories, the travelers search field `#tll-trav-q` (`axe-travelers-390.json`) and the Google mark `.tll-google-mark` on /signup (`axe-signup-390.json`). `aria-prohibited-attr` on `div.h3-cards[aria-label]` on home. `duplicate-id-aria` (2) on /share-photos. `form-field-multiple-labels` (2) on /signup. `skip-link` (1) on /account and /edit-profile, `aria-allowed-role` (1) on /account.
