## 2. The 45% stat

DRAFT. Not posted.

Hook: Nearly half of US pet owners have changed a trip because of the pet. The other half may simply not have admitted it yet.

Body:

- 45% of US pet owners changed, limited or canceled travel because they could not find a place that worked for their pet; for Gen Z it was 57% ([PetScreening, May 2026](https://www.petscreening.com/blog/from-pet-friendly-to-bookable-turning-demand-into-reservations)).
- Those are the trips that did not happen. On That Layover Life we collect the ones that did, and the ones where the pet stayed home with a sitter and still got the slide show.
- What would make "pet-friendly" actually bookable for you?

[story link in first comment]

## 3. The 82% fee stat

DRAFT. Not posted.

Hook: "Pet-friendly" turns out to be a pricing tier.

Body:

- An analysis of 1,000 "pet-friendly" hotels found 82% charge extra, and only 18% let dogs stay free ([Travel Industry Today, on Roch Dog's analysis](https://travelindustrytoday.com/barking-up-the-wrong-tree-are-pet-friendly-hotels-actually-friendly/); [Roch Dog](https://rochsociety.com/nobody-trusts-pet-friendly-anymore/)).
- The fee is not the problem. Surprise is.
- Paw Passport stories can include an optional "pet-friendly or pet-tolerant?" note: what the writer paid, the size rule, what made it welcoming. The writer's experience, dated, no stars.
- Hotels can earn a good note. They cannot buy one.

[story link in first comment]

## 4. The EU pet travel rule change (22 April 2026)

DRAFT. Not posted. Facts only, no advice.

Hook: The EU rewrote its rulebook for traveling with dogs, cats and ferrets this spring. The dog was not consulted.

Body:

- From 22 April 2026, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131 applies to the non-commercial movement of pet animals and replaces the rules in Regulation (EU) No 576/2013 ([EUR-Lex, Regulation 2026/131](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/131/oj); [FVE, new EU pet travel rules](https://uevp.fve.org/news/new-eu-pet-travel-rules-enter-into-force)). Last checked 31 Oct 2026.
- The playbook notes a cap of five pets per non-commercial movement. To check against the article text on EUR-Lex before posting.
- We are not vets or lawyers, so the post links the official text and stops there.
- Our EU pet guide is being checked line by line against the new rules before it goes on sale.

[link in first comment: the official EUR-Lex text]
