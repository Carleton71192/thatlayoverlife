## DSB (Denmark)

Official pages:

- English: [Travel with your pet](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/travel-with-pets/) (an older address, [/Travel-with-your-pet/](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/Travel-with-your-pet/), also shows up in search; check which one is live)
- Danish: [Du må gerne medbringe din hund eller kæledyr](https://www.dsb.dk/find-produkter-og-services/medbringe-din-hund-eller-kaledyr/)
- Danish, dog ticket: [Billet til hund (børnebillet)](https://www.dsb.dk/find-produkter-og-services/billet-til-hund-bornebillet)
- English, international trains: [Can I bring my dog on international trains?](https://www.dsb.dk/en/kundeservice/sporgsmal-og-svar/can-i-bring-my-dog-on-international-trains)

What DSB says (last checked 31 Oct 2026):

| Rule, in DSB's words as closely as we can | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Small pets in a carrier or cage travel free of charge. | [DSB, Travel with your pet](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/travel-with-pets/) | Matches the brief. Confirm on the page. |
| Larger dogs need a child ticket. | [DSB, Travel with your pet](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/travel-with-pets/); [DSB, Billet til hund](https://www.dsb.dk/find-produkter-og-services/billet-til-hund-bornebillet) | Matches the brief. Confirm on the page. |
| Larger dogs must be on a leash and stay on the floor for the whole journey. | [DSB, Travel with your pet](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/travel-with-pets/) | Matches the brief. Confirm on the page. |
| DSB 1' and the Quiet Zone are always animal-free areas (DSB gives allergies as the reason). | [DSB, Du må gerne medbringe din hund](https://www.dsb.dk/find-produkter-og-services/medbringe-din-hund-eller-kaledyr/) | Matches the brief, with the exception in the next row. |
| Guide dogs and service dogs travel free ("Rejser du med servicehund eller førerhund, er det gratis"). | [DSB, Du må gerne medbringe din hund](https://www.dsb.dk/find-produkter-og-services/medbringe-din-hund-eller-kaledyr/) | Matches the brief. Confirm which DSB page carries this line. |
| Service and guide dogs may be in the animal-free areas of DSB 1', but not in the DSB 1' Quiet Zone. | [DSB, Du må gerne medbringe din hund](https://www.dsb.dk/find-produkter-og-services/medbringe-din-hund-eller-kaledyr/) | FLAG: differs from the brief. The brief said "no pets in DSB 1' or the quiet zone" with no exception. DSB's page makes an exception for service and guide dogs in DSB 1' (not its Quiet Zone). One search summary of the English page worded this the opposite way, so read the page itself before publishing. |
| Look for the pet pictograms; some trains have sections for pets. | [DSB, Travel with your pet](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/travel-with-pets/) | Not in the brief. Confirm on the page. |
| If the seats face each other, you must reserve the seat opposite your own. You can also buy a space for the dog. | [DSB, Travel with your pet](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/travel-with-pets/) | Not in the brief. Confirm on the page. |
| Dogs are checked in on your Rejsekort. | [DSB, Travel with your pet](https://www.dsb.dk/en/tickets-and-services/travel-with-pets/) | Not in the brief. Confirm on the page, and check the product name is still current. |
| Rules for international journeys may differ; check the rules for each country. | [DSB, international trains](https://www.dsb.dk/en/kundeservice/sporgsmal-og-svar/can-i-bring-my-dog-on-international-trains) | Not in the brief. Confirm on the page. |
