# Pet travel pros: outreach note and the PAW flow

DRAFT for Nancy. Do not send. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.

## Part 1. Outreach note to small pet travel creators

Subject: Would you write for "From the Pros: Pet travel pros"?

Hi [first name],

I am Nancy, and I run That Layover Life (www.thatlayover.life), a free travel storytelling site where members keep a map of where they have been, collect stamps and write first-person stories. Paw Passport is our pet side: the pet's own stamps, firsts and slide show. Our test pilot is a dog who now has more stamps than most of my relatives.

I have been enjoying [specific thing they made: a post, a trip, a tip], and I would love to invite you to write a piece for a new category, "From the Pros: Pet travel pros."

What it would look like:

- A first-person story about a trip with your pet: what went well, what did not, what you would tell the next owner.
- Your byline, your photos (if you want them), your words. I edit lightly and send you the final version before it goes live.
- Your experience only, no vet, medical or legal advice. If you mention a rule, we link the official source with a "last checked" date.

How links work:

- One link, anchored on your name or your brand.
- If the piece is paid or the trip was hosted, the link carries rel="sponsored" and the story says so.
- Until I have read your first piece, the link carries rel="ugc nofollow". That is a standard step for every new writer, not a judgment.
- I will never ask you to link back to That Layover Life. If you want to, lovely; if not, also lovely.

If you are ever in Copenhagen, the coffee is on me, and the dog will want to meet yours.

No pressure at all, and thank you for reading this far.

Warmly,
Nancy
That Layover Life

### Before sending (checklist for Nancy)

- Personalize the [first name] and [specific thing] lines. No mass sends.
- If the work is paid, a short written agreement comes first (scope, fee, rights, confidentiality).
- Check whether the creator is a business; if so, disclose any payment or hosting on the story.
- Keep a note of who said no, so nobody gets asked twice.

## Part 2. ManyChat "PAW" comment-to-DM flow (Nancy sets this up herself)

What it does: when someone comments the keyword PAW on a TLL Instagram post, they get one direct message with the link to the Paw Passport page.

Setup notes:

- Trigger: a comment containing the keyword PAW (match the whole word, any case) on the chosen posts.
- Action: send one DM. Only one. No follow-up sequence, no reminders.
- DM text (draft):

  "Here is Paw Passport, where pets keep their own stamps, firsts and slide show: https://www.thatlayover.life/the-paw-passport. Thanks for the comment. The dog says hi."

- No prices in the DM. No discount codes. No "buy now."
- Optional public reply to the comment: "Sent you the link." Nothing more.
- Do not collect email or phone numbers in this flow.
- Test it once from a second account before turning it on, and check that the link opens the right page.
