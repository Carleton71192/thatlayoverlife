# Coffee with International House Copenhagen: one-page note

For Nancy to bring along. Draft, 31 Oct 2026. No agreement or joint work with International House Copenhagen exists; this note is a conversation starter.

## What That Layover Life is, in one line

A free travel storytelling site where members keep a map of the countries they have visited, stayed in or lived in, collect stamps for their pursuits, and write first-person stories.

## What the Living Library is

A page of TLL members who moved to a city and said yes to "open to coffee with newcomers." Everyone on it opted in, and can opt out at any time.

Each card shows:

- First name
- Home base city, only if they chose to show it
- Years there
- Places they know well
- Languages
- Interests
- Their stories on TLL

What the cards never show: email, phone, social handles or any other contact details.

How it works:

- "Get in touch" goes through a relay on TLL, and only after the newcomer signs in. Nobody's inbox is exposed.
- Cards appear in shuffled order, so nobody is on top by default.
- No paid placement. Nobody can buy a better spot.
- Every card has a report link.

## How it fits next to what IH already does

International House Copenhagen runs the official welcome: registration, career programs and regular welcome events ([International House Copenhagen](https://ihcph.kk.dk)). TLL does not touch paperwork and gives no legal, tax or immigration advice.

International Citizen Days has run a "Living Library" where newcomers borrow a "book," meaning a conversation with an experienced international resident ([AmCham Denmark, International Citizen Day](https://amcham.dk/events/partner-event-international-citizen-day/); [AmCham Denmark, International Citizen Days 2021](https://amcham.dk/news/international-citizen-day-2021/)). International Citizen Days is organized by International House Copenhagen ([International Citizen Days](https://www.icdays.kk.dk/)). Search did not confirm a Living Library in the 2026 program, so ask rather than assume.

Where TLL might help, as ideas to talk through:

- After the welcome event: a place newcomers can find people, and stories, in the weeks and months that follow.
- Year-round: the same idea as the Living Library, open on a Tuesday in February when no event is on.
- Stories that stay: first-person accounts of the first friend, the partner who moved too, and moving back, dated and still there next year.

## Questions to ask

- What do newcomers ask for after the welcome event that IH cannot offer?
- Would a TLL talk or table at International Citizen Days be of interest in future years?
- Are there rules IH would want respected if TLL ever mentioned IH events on the site?

## What Nancy is not asking for

Money, a logo on the site, or an endorsement. Just coffee, and maybe a second one.
