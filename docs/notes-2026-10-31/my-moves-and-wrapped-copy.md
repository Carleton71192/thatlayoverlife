## Living Library: "Borrow a book" explainer

Heading: Borrow a book

Body:

Every card here is a member who moved to this city and said yes to coffee with newcomers. Think of them as a book you can borrow for an hour.

Good things to ask:

- How did you make your first friend here?
- Which neighborhood felt like home, and why?
- What do you wish you had known in your first month?
- Where do you take visitors who have already seen everything?

Please do not ask:

- For legal, immigration or tax advice. Our books are people, not lawyers or accountants. For rules, use the official sources linked on our guides.
- For their address, workplace or where they are right now.
- For favors they did not offer, like a job, a flat or a reference.

How "Get in touch" works: sign in, write a short note, and we pass it on. Your book decides whether to reply. Nobody's contact details are on the cards.

How to say thank you: buy them the coffee, and if you want to thank That Layover Life too, you can become a supporter on Ko-fi [Ko-fi link]. Members are never paid for their time through TLL, and please do not offer them money.

## The Partner Who Moved Too: submit-form intro

Option A:

Moved because your partner's job did? This series is yours. Tell us what the move did to your work, your days and your sense of self, in your own words. A published story carries your full byline, so it is also a piece you can point to when someone asks about the gap on your CV.

Option B:

Your partner got the job. You got the moving boxes. Write the story of what came next. We publish it with your name and credit, and you can link to it from your CV or profile, because a published, credited story is a portfolio piece.

Small print under either option: Name your employer, or your partner's, only if you want to. We show city or country only.
