All three are DRAFTS for Nancy. Nothing has been posted. Each one links a TLL story in the first comment, and only a story whose writer has said yes.

Rules for every post: no immigration politics, no legal or tax advice, city or country only, no children's names, employers named only with permission. The joke is always on us or on the moving boxes.

## 1. The First Friend (Copenhagen)

DRAFT. Not posted.

Hook: In a 2023 expat survey, Denmark came last for making local friends. I took that personally, and then I took it to coffee.

Body:

- In InterNations' Expat Insider 2023 survey, 66% of respondents in Denmark said it was hard to make local friends, against a 36% global average ([The Local Denmark](https://www.thelocal.dk/20230711/denmark-ranked-hardest-nordic-country-for-foreigners-to-settle-in), reporting InterNations' Expat Insider 2023).
- Worth saying: the survey is promoted inside InterNations' own community, so it is a self-selected sample, not a census.
- Still, anyone who has stood alone holding a cardamom bun at a party knows the feeling.
- On That Layover Life, people who moved tell how they actually made their first friend in a new city. Copenhagen goes first.
- What finally worked for you, wherever you moved?

## 2. The Partner Who Moved Too

DRAFT. Not posted. Use a story only with the writer's permission.

Hook: When a family moves for one job, the other career often goes in a moving box. Usually the one labeled "misc."

Body:

- ECA International found that 90% of global businesses do not actively help an accompanying partner find work ([ECA International, Benefits for International Assignments Survey 2025, press release](https://eca.global/Content/Collateral/Press%20Releases/PressRelease-8Sept25-BenefitsforInternationalAssignments(BIA)Survey2025.pdf)).
